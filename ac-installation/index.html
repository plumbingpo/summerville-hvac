Why Hire a Professional for AC Installation?

The worst installation mistakes are the ones you cannot see at startup. Skip a proper vacuum, and moisture left in the line set turns to acid that attacks the compressor over the next few summers. Guess at the refrigerant charge instead of weighing it in, and the system wastes efficiency every hour it runs. Pick the size by rule of thumb, and an oversized unit short-cycles toward an early failure while the house stays humid. Each of these problems shows up later as the very repair bills and high energy costs a new system was supposed to end.

The steps that make equipment last are not flashy, and our technicians do every one of them. The line set is pressure-tested with nitrogen before evacuation. The vacuum is verified on a micron gauge. The charge is weighed in, then confirmed against superheat and subcooling. The condensate drain gets a proper trap and overflow protection, and airflow is measured with static pressure instead of a hand over a vent. Refrigerant work is also federally regulated and requires recovery and evacuation equipment you will not find at a hardware store. The payoff is fifteen quiet years of cooling, which is exactly what we commission each system to deliver.

Maintenance Tips for AC Installation

A little routine maintenance goes a long way toward avoiding a mid-season breakdown. Follow these tips from our experienced technicians:

  • Avoid huge thermostat setbacks, which force long, hard runs during the hottest hours
  • Keep the outdoor unit rinsed clean and clear of vegetation on all sides
  • Fix minor problems early, since a weak capacitor found in spring will not strand you in summer
  • Start a regular filter schedule on day one to protect the new coil from restricted airflow
  • Keep supply and return vents open to preserve the airflow balance set at commissioning
  • Give the condensate line a spring flush so a clog never shuts the system down
  • Have a technician check the system every spring so charge and amp draws stay where they were set
